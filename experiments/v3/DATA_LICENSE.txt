The ChEMBL-derived activity identifiers, molecule identifiers, assay/document
identifiers, pK observations, source metadata and corresponding adapted tables
in affinity.json, affinity_curated.json, affinity_sources.json, initial_affinity_implementation/*.json,
and their displayed table/figure equivalents in report.html are provided under
that same CC BY-SA 3.0 license. This notice does not change the source-code license.

Adaptations by the DMease project: human single-protein and assay-quality filters;
standardized molecular parents; median aggregation by compound, target and
endpoint; nM to pK conversion; fixed scaffold split; descriptor-based regression
and error summaries. The first execution's RDKit ring-information issue and its
correction are documented in affinity_implementation_amendment.json. ChEMBL does
not endorse the DMease models or their clinical use.

Other sources remain separately attributed: TCMEval-SDT data CC BY 4.0 per its
authors; TCM-SD data CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 per its author's repository; Qwen2.5-1.5B-
Instruct model Apache-2.0. Their raw clinical text and model weights are not
redistributed in this directory. See data/README.md for citations and scope.
