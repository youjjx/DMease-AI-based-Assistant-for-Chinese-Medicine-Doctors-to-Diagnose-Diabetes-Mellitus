identifier_links.json, identity_audit.json and the herb membership and graph
feature portions of molecular_bundle.json/planning_features.json are scoped
research-derived identifier/feature views, not complete database dumps. They
retain explicit source identifiers, source URLs, exclusions and provenance.
No general redistribution license was identified on the SymMap/TCMSP download
pages. The repository's software license does not grant new rights over those
source databases; retain attribution and consult their terms for downstream use.
Raw catalogues, site responses and full SQLite databases remain outside Git.

PubChem, National Center for Biotechnology Information, U.S. National Library
of Medicine. structures.json and structure fields in molecular_bundle.json
were retrieved using the official PUG REST CID property interface. Each record
retains its PubChem CID, property fields, retrieval request and response hash.
https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/pug-rest-tutorial
https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/policy-and-guidelines
https://pubchem.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/docs/downloads
PubChem compiles data from contributors; this notice does not replace any
applicable contributor terms or imply that every PubChem annotation is CC0.
Only structure/property records needed for this study are included.

This applies to affinity_evaluation.json, affinity_models.json, measured and
predicted affinity portions of molecular_bundle.json, unsupported_affinities.json
and corresponding adapted affinity tables/figures. The underlying measured
snapshot is ../v3/affinity_curated.json, not newly collected measurements.
Adaptations: replicate-conflict filtering, identity joins, global document and
scaffold grouping, leave-component-out regression, applicability gating,
uncertainty bands and monotonic evidence indices. No clinical efficacy or
endorsement by source providers is implied.

Planning requests are engineering retrieval inputs, not patient records or
pharmacological contraindication labels. Checkpoints contain learned parameters
and data/code provenance. UI verification uses explicitly nonclinical engineering
fixtures; real patient narratives and account secrets are not distributed.

These notices preserve source-specific rights separately from the project's
